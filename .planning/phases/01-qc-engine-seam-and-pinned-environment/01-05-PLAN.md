<objective>
Move the remaining three QC layers — Settlement, Takeoffpoint and Boundary — into `qc/`, and
rewire `app.py` to call all three across the seam.

Purpose: ENGINE-05 requires all QC layers to live in `qc/`, and this is the bulk of what is
left. These three layers introduce no identifier change at all: all thirteen of their
identifiers were already unique and are frozen by D-04. That makes this the phase's cleanest
relocation, and also the one where a silent behaviour change would be hardest to notice, since
nothing about the output is supposed to differ.
Output: three new layer modules, an `app.py` reduced to orchestration for the QC layers, and
three parity tests proving nothing changed.
</objective>
