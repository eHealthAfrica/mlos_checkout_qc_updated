<objective>
Move the MLoS layer into `qc/` and give the eleven checks that share two identifiers their own
field-named identifiers.

Purpose: this is where the phase's headline defect closes. Rule 11's loop emits three checks all
numbered 11 and rule 12's loop emits eight all numbered 12, so eleven checks carry an identifier
that names more than one thing, on screen and in every export. The same eight lines also carry
the ordering defect, because `YN_NA_COLS` was a set literal — fixed in plan 03's `qc/domain.py`,
consumed here. This is the largest and highest-risk relocation in the phase: 160 lines of rule
logic that must come across unchanged while eleven identifiers deliberately change.
Output: `qc/mlos.py` with 25 registered identifiers, an `app.py` that calls it across the seam,
and two tests pinning both the identifier contract and the unchanged behaviour.
</objective>
