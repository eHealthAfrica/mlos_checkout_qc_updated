<objective>
Close the phase: move the last pure function behind the seam, prove emission order is stable
across processes, and make the registry, the engine and the in-app reference agree with each
other by test rather than by convention.

Purpose: ENGINE-09's symptom is report reproducibility, but its cause is here — a set literal
iterated to emit eight checks, plus nothing structural stopping a future contributor from
declaring another one. Plan 03 converted the collection to a tuple and added the registry
re-sort; this plan proves both halves hold across process restarts, and closes the identity loop
with the invariant that nothing is emitted that was not registered and nothing is registered
that is not emitted.
Output: `qc/autocorrect.py`, an updated in-app rules reference, and three standing invariant
tests.
</objective>
