<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `pluralization` on "the emailed summary reports Settlement layer counts alongside
the other four layers" (ROADMAP success criterion 4).

**Noun now primary:** the *per-layer summary* — a `LayerSummary` record keyed by the registry's
own layer name — not the individual named layer count. The four separately named row counts
threaded as positional arguments into `send_qc_email` and `build_excel_report`
(`mlos_fail_rows`, `tp_fail_rows`, `boundary_fail_rows`, `schema_detail`) are demoted to a
detail read off that record.

**Decision: `promote`.** Adding a fifth positional argument for Settlement is what produced
F11 in the first place — the signature grew per layer, and the one layer nobody added never
appeared. `build_summary` takes a `dict[str, tuple[list, DataFrame]]` keyed by layer name and
returns one `QcSummary` holding a `LayerSummary` per layer; every presentation site iterates
`summary.layers` rather than naming layers one by one. A sixth layer becomes one dict entry
and appears at every site with no signature change.

**Companion invariant test (added, plan 04 task 1):** every layer name the registry knows
appears in every presentation site — the on-screen score table, the report's combined summary
sheet and the email body — asserted against `qc.results` rather than a hand-kept list.
</assumption_delta_decision>
