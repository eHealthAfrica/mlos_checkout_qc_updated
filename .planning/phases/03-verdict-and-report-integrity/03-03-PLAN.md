<objective>
Make the emailed summary describe all five layers, carry the verdict in its subject, and be
built by a pure function every test can call. Closes VERDICT-03 (F11 — today's body has no
Settlement line at all) and finishes VERDICT-04's on-screen story by reshaping the Generate
Report tab's three-layer breakdown to five layers with the distinct-row counts.

Purpose: an officer who reads only the email must see the same verdict and the same numbers
as an officer who opens the workbook. A layer missing from the body reads as a layer with
nothing wrong.
Output: `qc.report.build_email_subject_and_body`; `send_qc_email` reduced to an SMTP sender;
a five-layer Generate Report tab.
</objective>
